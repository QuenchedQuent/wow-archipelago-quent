# World of Warcraft

## What does randomization do?

Your character starts in Northshire Valley with almost no class abilities and
cannot leave the valley. Completing quests, reaching levels, exploring subzones
and killing rare mobs sends checks. Zone access keys and class spells are shuffled
into the multiworld.

## What is the goal?

Reach the configured goal level.

## Requirements

A 3.3.5 AzerothCore private server with Eluna, the Archipelago bridge client,
and the Archipelago in-game addon. See the setup guide.
